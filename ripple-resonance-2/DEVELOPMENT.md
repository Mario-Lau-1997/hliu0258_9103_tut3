# Development stages

The common painted background is applied to every snapshot in this refreshed set.

## Stage 1: Source controls

Add sources with a button or the A key; choose overlapping sources from a list. Match pointer IDs when ending a drag.

## Stage 2: Undo and redo

Capture small scene snapshots. Group continuous gestures into one edit. Restore settings, clear redo after a new edit, and cap the stack at 30.

The snapshots do not change the author or dates of any existing Git commits.
